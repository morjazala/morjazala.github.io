MORJA is for you; the dreamer, the adventurer, the one who wears their story with pride. Each upcycled creation carries a past, stitched into something new. With every pattern mix and patchwork detail, you’re not just wearing clothes, you’re wearing emotions, memories, and a belief in a more thoughtful way of living.

Created by Zala, a designer who finds magic in reimagining the old, MORJA is her way of sharing warmth, joy, and a love for the beautifully imperfect. Thank you for being part of this colorful journey.

For any questions, (custom made) orders or interest in collaboration, I'll be happy to hear from you.

Give your pre-loved garments and fabrics a second life by passing them on to MORJA, where they'll be upcycled into new stories!

We are looking for:

  • Home textiles (tablecloths, bedsheets, curtains)
  • Lace, embroidery, and unique details
  • Cotton men’s shirts
  • Patterned, floral, or colourful fabrics
  • Linen and cotton materials
  • Pre-loved garments with unique textures or stories

How to donate: Drop off in person (by arrangement) or send by mail. Thank you for helping keep these materials in circulation!
