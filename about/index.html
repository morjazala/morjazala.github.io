MORJA is a Slovenian upcycling brand rooted in textile storytelling and the belief that forgotten materials deserve a new chapter. Through reconstruction and creative experimentation, it transforms vintage textiles, pre-loved garments and textile remnants into contemporary, one-of-a-kind pieces.

Founded by Zala Hrastar in her small workshop in rural Slovenia, MORJA explores the connection between craft, memory and material reuse. Each garment carries its own emotional landscape, brought together through colour, texture and pattern… like fragments of everyday life woven into a new story.

Beyond clothing, MORJA is an ongoing artistic practice exploring collage, photography, poetry and sound, always guided by curiosity and care for what already exists. At its core, MORJA celebrates slower ways of making and wearing, creating pieces that are not only worn, but remembered.

MORJA spreads kindness, colour and quiet optimism through sustainable design.
