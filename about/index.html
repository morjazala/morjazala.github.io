MORJA is a Slovenian brand that gives a second chance to the forgotten, dead-stock textiles, waste materials, and pre-loved second-hand clothes by upcycling and reconstructing them into new items to save them from landfills.

At the heart of MORJA are colourful patchworks of everyday emotions in a pattern mix.

All items are handmade by Zala Hrastar in her small home workshop in a tiny Slovenian village.

Morja is a patchwork of all her dreams, thoughts and beliefs. Morja spreads kindness, good vibrations, and positivity and encourages people to live more environmentally conscious with Mother Earth and constantly improve and grow.

Zala aims to produce quality items with lots of patience, love and respect for the material that carries a thousand stories and waits with you for a thousand more.

She is currently interested in combining waste textiles, digital & paper collage illustrations, photography, poetry and melodies into a new life for new possibilities and new smiles.
