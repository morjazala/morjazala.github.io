Dano & Rene

Photo: Raw Vows

Ever since creating my first wedding dress for my sister in 2017, I’ve dreamed of having a little wedding atelier of my own, creating meaningful garments for such special occasions. So when Dano reached out after discovering my O Happy Day wedding capsule collection, I was beyond excited. Together, we spent an afternoon in my studio, surrounded by piles of vintage textiles, choosing fabrics that felt just right for their wedding. I loved the thought of bringing these once-loved materials into a new chapter of someone’s life.

From embroidered vintage tablecloths, vintage patterned fabrics, crochet pieces and second-hand garments, I created four custom pieces: a vibrant patchwork jacket and a shirt for Dano, a matching patchwork kimono for Rene to wear over her base white wedding dress, and a tiny matching collar for their daughter. Each garment brought together different textures, patterns and little traces of the past, reimagined into something entirely new.

Made to be worn, loved and lived in long after the wedding day… carrying a little piece of their story wherever life takes them.
