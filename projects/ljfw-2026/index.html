ROSENLUND

My first solo presentation at Ljubljana Fashion Week with ROSENLUND. A moment I had dreamed of, worked towards, and now find myself still trying to put into words.

I can’t express how grateful I am to everyone who came to experience the runway with me. To those who stopped me afterwards with kind words, hugs, encouragement and support … you made me feel so incredibly loved. I was overwhelmed with emotion, and yes, I cried a lot. Thank you, from the bottom of my heart.

I wouldn’t be here without my family and friends. The people who believe in me, stand by me, and remind me why I do what I do. We inspire each other to grow, to be better, to keep creating and, most importantly, to simply be. Even in challenging times, something beautiful can grow.

And I think that is, in many ways, what ROSENLUND is about. A community of people, each bringing their own character, knowledge, hands and imagination to the table. Everyone has their own place and their own role, and it is through these connections that something bigger begins to take shape. A collection becomes more than just clothes. It becomes a shared story.

Looking back at this week, I feel incredibly lucky to be surrounded by so many people who care, create, encourage and dream alongside me. Thank you for being part of this chapter, for showing up, and for reminding me that none of us creates alone.

With a full heart, Zala ♡
