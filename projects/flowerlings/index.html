FLOWERLINGS

Photo: Zala Hrastar

FLOWERLINGS is a textile project developed as part of the BIEN Mini Textile Incubator, a mentorship programme exploring contemporary interpretations of textile heritage and archival patterns.

While researching the rich textile printing history of city Kranj and exploring archival patterns, I found myself drawn to flowers everywhere. I wanted to create my own interpretation of floral prints; something a little raw, organic and imperfect, with the playful and poetic character of MORJA. Inspired by traditional folk floral ornamentation, crumbling walls and old wallpapers, I began exploring the meeting point between delicate floral motifs and decaying surfaces.

The process started analog, using cyanotype technique with vintage crochet doilies. Their delicate structures became the starting point for a series of textures and impressions. I then digitised the textile imprints and continued experimenting through digital manipulation, deconstruction and layering, developing eight original repeat patterns.

In contrast to the tiny repeats often found in vintage textiles, I decided to enlarge the scale of the patterns, allowing the motifs to unfold more freely across the fabric. The final designs were translated into wearable pieces, imagined as tops and silk scarves.

FLOWERLINGS is part of **FIRST IMPRINT, a group exhibition** presenting the outcomes of the BIEN Mini Textile Incubator. The exhibition opened on 7th November 2026 at Layer House in Kranj and runs until 12th January 2027. Free entry.

All eight original prints were produced on fabric by TISKA Tekstina, who supported the project as its production sponsor.
