Slovenka

Photo: Zala Hrastar

For the Amazing Thailand Global Thai Silk Exhibition, I created a contemporary reinterpretation of Slovenian folk costume, translating its recognisable symbols, colours and textile traditions into modern silhouettes.

The look consists of two pieces: a black silk strappy dress with embroidered red carnation, and a bomber jacket that reinterprets the traditional white blouse and woven scarf as a single contemporary garment.

Through the combination of silk, vintage textiles, embroidery and reimagined forms, I wanted to explore how cultural heritage and personal memory can find new expression in contemporary fashion, allowing familiar elements of Slovenian folk costume to evolve while preserving the stories they carry.
