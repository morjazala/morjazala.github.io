LIGHT OF THE PAST

Photo: Zala Hrastar

Life and surroundings shape every individual in a different way, I was shaped by living in a small village, in a 6-member family. In April my parents celebrated the 30 year anniversary of their life together, and this photo series was made as a memory and as a gratitude for the family.

For the beginnings and for the future.

With the photos, I wanted to create a warm, home atmosphere, spirit of the past trough colour, and motives.

Photo series won 1st award at Čist FreeŠn x MagoaMag open call in year 2020 themed ‘’nostalgia’.
