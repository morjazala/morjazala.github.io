What if your wedding dress wasn’t meant for just one day?

I create one-of-a-kind wedding garments from vintage, pre-loved and family textiles: designed around you, your story and the life beyond your wedding day.

An embroidered tablecloth, old lace curtain or beloved linen can become your dress, shirt, jacket or another special piece.

You bring the story. I give it a new form.

Discover how it works

01 → Your textiles

Bring your own vintage, pre-loved or family textiles. If you don’t have anything suitable, we can also explore materials from my collection.

02 → Your story

We talk about what you’re looking for, how you imagine wearing the garment and which textiles or details are meaningful to you.

03 → The design

I develop the design around your wishes, your body and the character of the textiles you’ve chosen.

04 → The making

Your garment is handmade in my studio, with fittings and adjustments along the way. Ideally, we begin around 6 months before your wedding, but I can also take on commissions with a minimum of 3 months’ notice, depending on the project.

05 → Beyond the wedding

The goal is to create a garment that has a life beyond the wedding: to be worn, loved and lived in.

FOR ALL THE HAPPY DAYS TO COME

Your wedding day is only one of them.

Let’s create something you’ll want to wear long after the celebrations are over. A piece made from your story, for your wedding day, and all the happy days still to come.

You don’t need a wedding to give precious textiles a new life.

Maybe you’d like to create something for a different occasion or simply turn something you’ve been holding onto for years into a piece you can enjoy again.

And if you have textiles you no longer need but would love to see used again, you’re welcome to get in touch about donating them to MORJA.

Give your pre-loved garments and fabrics a second life by passing them on to MORJA, where they'll be upcycled into new stories!

We are looking for:

  • Home textiles (tablecloths, bedsheets, curtains)
  • Lace, embroidery, and unique details
  • Cotton men’s shirts
  • Patterned, floral, or colourful fabrics
  • Linen and cotton materials
  • Pre-loved garments with unique textures or stories

How to donate: Drop off in person (by arrangement) or send by mail. Thank you for helping keep these materials in circulation!
